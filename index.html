[ COMMON INQUIRIES ]

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding security deposits, rapid Bangalore delivery, and safety inspection protocols.

What are the security deposit requirements and how quickly are they refunded? +
Security deposits are standard in heavy equipment rentals and are calculated based on material tonnage and hire duration. For corporate and registered contractors, we accept refundable security deposits or pre-agreed bank guarantees. Upon completion of your project and return of materials to our RT Nagar depot, our team conducts a joint count, and your deposit balance is refunded via RTGS/NEFT or UPI within 24 to 48 hours without delay.
What are your delivery timelines across Bangalore and surrounding districts? +
With our central depot situated in RT Nagar, standard stock (MS Cuplock, Aluminium Mobile Towers, Acrow props, and shuttering plates) ordered before 11:00 AM qualifies for Same-Day Express Site Delivery across Bangalore (including Hebbal, Manyata, CBD, Whitefield, Peenya, and Electronic City). Heavy multi-tonnage consignments are delivered within 24 hours guaranteed. Outside Bangalore (Mysuru, Tumakuru, Hubballi), dispatch is scheduled within 48 hours.
What safety standards and inspection procedures do your scaffolding systems follow? +
All our steel scaffolding strictly adheres to BIS IS:2750, IS:1239, and OSHA 1926 standards. Every pipe returning to our yard undergoes ultrasonic wall thickness checks (guaranteeing 3.2mm structural wall thickness), straightness inspection, and weld testing. On site, when our certified riggers erect the scaffolding, we conduct daily safety checks and attach a visible Green Scafftag at the ladder base certifying the structure as 100% safe for civil work.
Do you offer certified erection manpower or material-only hire? +
We offer both flexible options: (1) Material-Only Rental where we deliver certified scaffolding components to your site for your internal team to assemble; and (2) Turnkey Supply & Erection where our certified scaffolding riggers handle complete assembly, structural wall-ties, toeboards, handrails, and final Scafftag handover.

[ HIRE POLICIES & GUIDELINES ]

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our security deposits, delivery fleets, inspection procedures, and Scafftag safety certifications.

What are your security deposit requirements and when is it refunded? +

Security deposits protect high-value capital equipment during transit and site usage. Deposits are calculated proportionally based on total material tonnage and projected rental tenure (typically equivalent to 1–2 months rental value). For established builders and corporate accounts, pre-agreed bank guarantees or corporate purchase orders are accepted.

Refund Procedure: When materials return to our RT Nagar yard, our supervisor conducts a joint tally check with your transport representative. Any outstanding rental dues or damage adjustments are calculated transparently, and the remaining security deposit is transferred via RTGS/NEFT or UPI within 24 to 48 hours with an official credit statement.

What are your delivery timelines across Bangalore and outside districts? +

We maintain dedicated delivery vehicles (heavy flatbed trucks and prompt commercial pick-ups) stationed at our Chamundi Nagar / RT Nagar depot:

  • Same-Day Express Dispatch: Orders confirmed before 11:00 AM qualify for same-day delivery across Bangalore (Hebbal, Manyata, CBD, Whitefield, Peenya, Electronic City).
  • Standard 24-Hour Guarantee: All standard consignments (MS Cuplock, Aluminium Towers, Acrow props) are delivered to your site within 24 hours.
  • Pan-Karnataka Projects: For sites in Mysuru, Tumakuru, Mandya, Hassan, Belagavi, or Hubballi, dispatch is coordinated within 48 hours.
What on-site inspection and safety procedures do you carry out? +

Safety is non-negotiable at PARRY'S. We enforce a 3-tier inspection protocol:

  • Yard Ultrasonic Quality Check: Every pipe returning from site is tested for wall thickness (minimum 3.2mm structural compliance), straightness, and crack-free welded cup joints. Substandard pieces are retired immediately.
  • Joint Unloading Verification: On arrival, our delivery team inspects the delivery challan alongside your site supervisor.
  • Scafftag Safety Handover: When our certified riggers perform the erection, we affix an official Scafftag at the main access point: Green Tag (Certified 100% Safe for Use with signed date & load capacity), Yellow Tag (Inspection or modification in progress), or Red Tag (Prohibited entry). Daily safety inspections are conducted following BIS IS:2750 and OSHA 1926 standards.
What is the minimum hire period and how do extensions work? +

For heavy construction scaffolding (MS Cuplock, Acrow props, shuttering plates), the minimum billing period is 30 days. For Aluminium Mobile Access Towers and event stage staging, we provide daily, weekly, and monthly rates. If your project timeline expands, extending your hire requires no fresh paperwork—simply notify our RT Nagar dispatch desk via WhatsApp or phone 48 hours prior. Billing continues on a prorated daily or monthly basis.

Do you provide scaffolding erection manpower or only material hire? +

We cater to both requirements:

  • Material-Only Supply: We deliver certified materials directly to your site. Your trained site labor handles assembly.
  • Turnkey Supply & Erection: Our certified rigging team arrives with full PPE (fall-arrest harnesses, shock-absorbing lanyards, helmets, safety boots) to erect, securely tie to structural walls, install toe-boards, and hand over with a Green Scafftag certificate.
What is your policy regarding damaged material or lost pins/couplers? +

Normal wear, weather patina, and minor cement dust are expected and never billed. However, heavily deformed pipes (run over by earthmovers), cut tubing, or missing couplers/pins are billed at standardized, open manufacturer rates printed on your delivery challan and deducted transparently from the security deposit upon joint reconciliation.

Do you issue official GST invoices for Input Tax Credit (ITC)? +

Yes. PARRY'S Scaffolding & Rentals is fully registered under Karnataka GST. Every rental consignment is invoiced with compliant 18% GST under HSN Code 7308 along with statutory E-way bills, allowing commercial builders and corporations to claim full Input Tax Credit.
